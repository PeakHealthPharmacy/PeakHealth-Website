Comprehensive Health Screening Support

Our screening test kits, combined with a presenting symptom assessment, can support a clearer understanding of your health and sensible next-step care. Private health screening is available directly at the pharmacy — no GP referral is needed. These simple screening tests come with pharmacist advice and next-step support, and may suit you if you want a better understanding of your health or are checking a specific concern.

Tests may include finger-prick blood tests, urine samples, stool samples and other specimen testing. Many in-store tests have results available in as little as 15 minutes. Home test kits include a follow-up consultation to discuss your results, with no hidden costs. Where clinically appropriate following pharmacist assessment, treatment may be provided by our prescribing pharmacists. Professional advice and GP referral for further investigations can also be arranged where appropriate.

Screenings are delivered from our pharmacy on Lochend Road, Easterhouse, serving Glasgow's East End and surrounding communities.

Why Health Screening Matters

Some health concerns may not cause obvious symptoms in the early stages. Regular checks can help identify risk markers and prompt timely next steps, and pharmacist support helps you understand your results and access appropriate care. Screening is not a replacement for urgent or emergency care. If you need urgent help, please see When to Seek Urgent Medical Help.

When to Seek Urgent Medical Help

Our health screening appointments are for routine private screening only — they are not suitable for emergencies or serious acute symptoms.

If you or someone else is experiencing any of the following, please seek urgent help immediately:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Severe shortness of breath
  • Signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty)
  • Severe or sudden abdominal pain
  • Collapse or loss of consciousness
  • Any other sudden or serious symptoms

Call 999 in an emergency. For urgent but non-emergency concerns, call NHS 111 or contact your GP urgently.

Do not delay urgent care to attend a screening appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a GP referral for a health screening?

No. You can book directly with us without seeing your GP first. Simply book online or call us on 0141 773 2003 and select Health Screenings from the booking menu.

How long does a health screening appointment take?

In-store tests typically take between 15 and 30 minutes, including your consultation and results discussion. Home test kit appointments involve a short initial visit to receive your kit, followed by a separate follow-up consultation to discuss your results. Both appointments are included in the price.

What is the difference between an in-store test and a home test kit?

In-store tests are carried out at the pharmacy — your pharmacist takes a small sample such as a finger-prick blood test and results are typically available within 15 minutes. Home test kits involve collecting a sample at home at your convenience. A follow-up consultation to discuss your results is included with all home kit services at no extra charge.

Can screening diagnose a condition?

Screening tests help identify risk markers or possible concerns — they are not the same as a full clinical diagnosis. Where a result suggests a concern, your pharmacist will discuss next steps clearly, which may include GP follow-up, further investigation or treatment where clinically appropriate.

Do I need to prepare for any tests?

For the blood glucose test, a fasting measurement is preferred for accuracy — your pharmacist will advise when you book. For home test kits, your pharmacist will explain how to collect your sample at your initial appointment. No specific preparation is needed for most other in-store tests.

What happens if my result needs further follow-up?

Your pharmacist will explain your results and advise clearly on the most appropriate next steps. Where GP referral or further investigation is recommended, your pharmacist will help signpost you in the right direction. Where treatment is clinically appropriate, it may be provided by our prescribing pharmacist at the same appointment.

Are health screening appointments confidential?

Yes. Your appointment is handled confidentially and respectfully in line with our professional and legal responsibilities.

Is there a separate consultation fee?

No. The price of each screening includes your pharmacist consultation, the test or home kit, and any included follow-up appointment. There are no hidden fees. Pricing is available on our booking menu before you confirm your appointment.
